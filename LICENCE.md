## The approach is free to follow

What is reserved above is this code. The approach it demonstrates is not
restricted by this licence: keeping each kind of decision in one place,
documenting every component beside its source, binding composition rules to
tokens, naming what enforces each convention, recording a product's page types
beside its screens, and checking all of it while the code is written. The
README's "The framework, and the example" sets out that structure. Building a
design system of your own to it, in your own code, needs no permission.
